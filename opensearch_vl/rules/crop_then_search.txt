**Crop-then-search rule (identifying what the image shows)**: when the question depends on identifying a specific thing in the image (a species, breed, building, landmark, artwork, person, product or vehicle) and `image_search` on the whole image (`img_1`) does not clearly name it (the results are unrelated, only a generic category, or about something else), first `crop` the main subject tightly (just the object, animal, plant, building or person the question is about), then call `image_search` on the cropped image (e.g. `{"url": "img_2"}`) before moving on to `text_search`.
